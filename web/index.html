How it works

3 signal layers
  1. Lexical + signals. Unicode-aware character and word n-grams learn obfuscated links, transliterated scams, and code-mixing. 100 engineered features add URL, payment, urgency, OTP, and formatting evidence.
  2. Multilingual semantics. A frozen MiniLM multilingual encoder catches paraphrases, cross-language scam scripts, and offers without obvious keywords. A calibrated linear head scores the embedding.
  3. Calibration. A validation-fit stacker blends both branches and the heuristic score, then a precision-constrained threshold produces NORMAL, SUSPICIOUS, or SPAM.

The old English-only DeBERTa-v3-extra-small H5 baseline is intentionally not used for multilingual production.
